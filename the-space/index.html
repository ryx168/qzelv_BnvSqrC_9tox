The Space

The Body Spirit Centre offers a workshop space in my home. The workshop space is (12ft x 40ft ) on ground level with a large foyer entrance. There is an adjoining room (10ft x 20ft) that can be used with the main group room, only for multiple day workshops.

Thank you for considering the Body Spirit Centre.

The cost for the group room is:

– $15.00 per hour
– $ 50.00 per night (6pm-10pm)
– $100.00 per day (9am-6pm) plus $25.00 per night
(if multiple days)

The cost for the adjoining room is:

-$ 50.00 per day / night included

* There are sleeping accommodations available $25.00 per night per person

There are no meals available on property, however, the location is one block away from the village centre and some of Gabriola’s finest restaurants. I provide filtered water,a large urn for hot water and a fridge for food, all in the group room. Let me know if you have any special requests or needs for your workshop.
